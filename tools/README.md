# tools/ — builder inputs (dev/agent notes)

Purpose: everything the German pack build reads or writes, other than the
shared builder itself (`engine/tools/packbuilder`, `langs/de.py`). Read this
before touching any file here; it says what is hand-maintained vs generated.

## Files

- `build_pack.py` — shim. Runs `python3 -m packbuilder build --lang de --repo .`
  against `engine/tools` (or `$PACKBUILDER_PATH`). Hand-maintained, rarely changes.
- `requirements.txt` — hand-maintained. Shared builder deps plus the German
  spaCy model (`de_core_news_sm`).
- `gloss_overrides.json` — hand-maintained. About 180 hand gloss fixes for
  high-frequency words (keyed `"lemma|pos"`, lowercase lemma).
- `forced_a1.txt` — hand-maintained. A1 core word list forced into A1 (closed
  sets live in `langs/de.py` instead).
- `passages_src.json` — hand-maintained. Source text for the 60 reading
  passages; `packbuilder passages` builds `pack/passages.json` from it.
- `id_map_v1.json` — generated, then frozen. Maps the builder's internal
  lowercase (lemma, POS) key (`haus|noun`, `beamter|noun` — not the displayed
  words.json lemma) to a fixed word id across rebuilds. Never hand-edit;
  never renumber. `.cache/derived/de_idkeys.json` holds the current build's
  keys for comparison.
- `REPORT.md` — generated on every full build by `build_pack.py`. Everything
  outside the `manual` markers is regenerated; the manual section (QA
  verdicts, check output) is preserved across runs.
- `REPORT_passages.md` — generated by `packbuilder passages`. Per-passage
  coverage numbers and QA notes.

## Rebuild

See CLAUDE.md's "Commands" section for the pinned invocations. Sources
download once into `.cache/` (gitignored) and are reused; the build is
deterministic, so a cached re-run reproduces byte-identical `pack/*.json`.
The spaCy tagging pass over the English-translated Tatoeba sentences runs
once and is cached under `.cache/derived/`.

## German-specific rules

The builder's German module (`langs/de.py`) handles what the shared pipeline
cannot guess:

- **Nouns** show their article (`das Haus`); `alt[0]` is the bare noun, so
  typing either is accepted. Gender and plural come from Wiktionary. The
  plural is shown under the word (`pl. Häuser`, `no pl.`, `pl. only`).
  Adjectival nouns show the weak form with both articles (`der/die Angestellte`).
- **Capitalisation.** The subtitle frequency list is lowercased, so lemmas
  match in lowercase; noun case is restored from the part of speech.
  Homographs (essen / das Essen) split by POS in the tagged corpus.
- **Separable verbs** are single lemmas (`anfangen`). When split in a
  sentence ("Er fängt morgen an."), the particle is rejoined with the
  clause's finite verb before counting/linking, only when Wiktionary has the
  joined verb.
- **Sie vs sie** are separate entries; a capitalised Sie mid-sentence is
  formal, and at sentence-start the English translation picks "you" vs
  "she"/"they".
- **Tenses.** A1/A2 sentences use present tense, Perfekt, and Präteritum of
  sein/haben/modals only; other Präteritum sentences are kept for B1.
- **Typing.** Case-insensitive; umlauts lenient at A1/A2 (`a`=`ä`,
  ae/oe/ue/ss accepted), strict from B1. A fold-only match is rejected when
  it spells another pack word instead: `schon`/`schön` and `zahlen`/`zählen`
  are each distinguished, in both directions.
- Compounds stay single lemmas, never decomposed. Modal verbs are drilled as
  content words.

## Reading passages

`packbuilder passages .` builds `pack/passages.json` from `passages_src.json`
and writes `REPORT_passages.md`. It links word ids the same way as the
example sentences, and enforces in-pack coverage of at least 95% at A1/A2
and 93% at B1, plus a level budget (an A1 passage may use at most 3 A2 words
and no B1 words; an A2 passage at most 3 B1 words). German-specific passage
linker rules (finite-verb-next-to-subject disambiguation, salutation lieb
vs. lieben, meisten/Meister guard, gehört after haben, am liebsten/lieber,
als/wie clause-role rules, alle-family routing, zu/um preposition-vs-
infinitive-marker rules, kennenlernen joining, capitalised-noun-as-name
overrides) live in `langs/de.py`'s passage hooks, not in this repo — see
`../TODO.md`. Regenerate `pack/*.js` afterward (jsonify_pack.py appends
passages into sentences.js).

## Levels are a frequency proxy, not CEFR

A probe of 41 hand-picked words puts every expected-A1 word in A1 and 36 of
41 within one level, but frequent B1 words such as obwohl and Meinung land
in A2 because subtitle frequency ranks some everyday written-register words
past the cut. 39 such words (Supermarkt, Miete, Fahrkarte, regnen, Schnee,
and others) are kept by hand at B1 via `keep_keys` in `langs/de.py`,
displacing 39 film-register words near the cut (weapons, crime, slang).
Details and the full word lists are in `../TODO.md` and `REPORT.md`.
