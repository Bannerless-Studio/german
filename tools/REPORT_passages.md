## Manual notes (2026-09-24, QA polish round)

- **Source and build.** The 60 passages are hand-written for this pack in
  `tools/passages_src.json`. `python3 -m packbuilder passages .` writes
  `pack/passages.json` (with per-sentence `spans`) and this report. Two runs
  give byte-identical output. The builder is vocab-engine 3e1d3b9 plus the
  German patch in `.cache/pb/de_port.diff` (not yet in vocab-engine; local
  cache, not committed).
- **Builder port (from the first build).** `load_context` compares folded
  lemmas and replays `refill_unexampled`; a cached context re-binds the
  lexicon; a separable particle joined by `post_resolve` counts and links as
  its verb; an article lemma with no other pack word links the article entry;
  `passage_lemma_alias` maps vieler to viel and Chefin to Chef.
- **Link fixes (QA round).** Passage-only rules in `langs/de.py`
  (`_sense_guards`, on only under `packbuilder passages`; the corpus build
  keeps them off: a trial with them on changed 6 word ids):
  - finite verb next to a subject pronoun is the verb (ich heiße: heißen, not heiß);
  - "Liebe Kunden!" salutation is lieb, never the imperative of lieben;
  - die/am meisten links viel; a lowercase token's lemma fallback never lands
    on a noun (meisten was der Meister);
  - gehört before und after haben is hören;
  - am liebsten links gern; comparative lieber already did;
  - als with no comparison before it is w0086 (als Lehrer, als Kind), not "than";
  - wie opening an indirect question is w0059 "how", not "as";
  - alle/allen/aller link all, not alles (vor allem stays alles);
  - zu before a numeral, noun or article/pronoun is the preposition (bis zu achtzehn);
  - um with no zu-infinitive in its clause is the preposition (um den Sieg);
  - lernt/lernte ... kennen joins to kennenlernen;
  - capitalised pack nouns tagged as names link the noun (Meine Mutter Maria,
    seit letztem Sommer, Minuten Suche, Essen, Äpfel); given names and Herr/Frau X stay names;
  - a verb reading suppresses the surface interjection (Ich bitte Sie: bitten only).
  Scripted scan of passages.json: 0 sites in every QA class (A1-A11, C, D).
- **Same classes in sentences.json (report only, not rebuilt).** Re-linking all
  3408 sentences with the rules on changes: als role 24, indirect wie 22,
  allen 7, capitalised noun read as name 6, zu+NP 1, am liebsten 1, um+NP 1;
  die meisten 5 (resolution only, same link). heiße, Liebe, gehört,
  kennenlernen and bitte: 0. Applying them to sentences.json needs a pack
  rebuild with the rules enabled for the corpus, which changes 6 word ids.
- **Glosses (tools/gloss_overrides.json).** Added the senses the passages use:
  scheinen (shine), Achtung (attention), wohl (sich wohl fühlen), meinen
  (think), leiten (lead, run), unterhalten (sich: chat), vorstellen (sich
  etwas: imagine), Hals (throat), Geschichte (history), sicher (safe), dick
  (thick, swollen), Freund (boyfriend; Freundin girlfriend), nah (nächst-:
  next), früh (früher: formerly), um (for, about). words.json changed only in
  these 15 glosses; sentences.json is byte-identical.
- **Senses avoided.** Band, Tor, Karte as menu/ticket, Schloss as lock, an der
  Reihe, predicative zu, auf Wiedersehen, adverbial bestimmt, laut Musik, das
  stimmt and öfter stay avoided. Leiterin (only die Leiter "ladder" in the
  pack) became Chefin.
- **Everyday gaps.** The subtitle-based list lacks Supermarkt, Gemüse,
  regnen, Schnee, Jacke, Miete, Fahrkarte, Ausland, Nachteil, parken, Zucker,
  Koch, zurückschicken and others; passages are written around them. The only
  out-of-pack word is gleis (station announcement, p0033). Zucker was dropped
  from p0025; the actor in p0030 is fictional (Paul Hofmann).
- **Names.** Declared in `oop` with reason "name": mia (p0004), krüger
  (p0029), leas and walters (p0053). Herr Wolf (p0012) stays a name.
- **Question checks.** 285 questions: 143 mc, 142 tf (65 true). Correct mc
  positions 0/1/2/3 per level: A1 11/11/10/10, A2 13/13/12/12, B1 13/13/13/12.
  Correct option is the unique longest: A1 5/42, A2 10/50, B1 6/51. No mc key
  copies 5+ words of its passage; 4-word copies A1 2/42, A2 5/50, B1 2/51. No
  tf item shares a 5-word run with its passage; the one-word negation items
  (p0014, p0015, p0020) were rewritten. Answers that hinge on a number list
  the numeral in `words`. p0045 and p0059 items hinge on passage details, not
  common advice. No mc item has two options with the same meaning.
- **Residuals (low).** Idiom parts link separately: vor allem (alles), unter
  der Woche (Woche), erst einmal (erst + einmal), Danke schön (schön). The
  sentence-initial article in „Die meisten ... (p0043 s5) has no span.
- **Tenses.** A1 uses the present tense, with a little perfect in dialogue.
  A2 adds the perfect and some Präteritum beyond sein and haben (standen,
  konnte, gab), which is normal for A2 reading. B1 adds more Präteritum,
  Konjunktiv II, Futur, passive and reported speech.
